# Interview: airport duty manager, Port of Seattle

Label: role-play, not evidence. Fallback file, used when card 1 is unassigned. Copy this folder into `.copilot-tracking/dt/` before synthesis.

## Can see

Runway status, ground crews, gates. Radio to tower, fire and rescue, ground. The Titan Watch liaison in the room.

## Cannot see

Aircraft in the air. Fuel. Vespyra, except through the liaison.

## Questions and answers

**What happens in your room when the liaison says 19 minutes?**
I call the tower and ask what is on final. I call fire and rescue and tell them to stage. I open every gate I can. That is three calls and it takes four minutes before anyone outside the room is moving. Assume and label: four minutes.

**How many aircraft can you take?**
Two runways in this visibility. The tower says one a minute each. Ground side, I can clear a runway in ninety seconds if the crew is already there, three minutes if not. Twenty in fifteen minutes is the number I would plan on. I do not know if that is right.

**Who decides land versus divert?**
Not me. I take what lands. I would expect the FAA. I have never had an order from Titan Watch and would not know whether to obey one.

**What is the worst thing that happens on your side?**
An aircraft lands, Vespyra crosses the field, and it turns out on the ground was not safe. Nobody has told me whether it is. I have been assuming landed means done.

**What would you want from a tool?**
One list. What is landing, in what order, when, and whether there is anything unusual about it. The liaison's clock on the same screen, with how old it is.
