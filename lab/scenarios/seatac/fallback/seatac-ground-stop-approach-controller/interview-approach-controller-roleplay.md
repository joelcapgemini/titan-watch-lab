# Interview: TRACON approach controller, Sea-Tac

Label: role-play, not evidence. Fallback file, used when card 2 is unassigned. Copy this folder into `.copilot-tracking/dt/` before synthesis.

## Can see

Radar for every aircraft in approach airspace. Voice with each cockpit, one at a time. A large erratic primary return that is not squawking.

## Cannot see

Fuel. Vespyra's projected track. Runway readiness.

## Questions and answers

**What happens in the first four minutes after a ground stop?**
I stop feeding the final. The twelve already on it land or go around. The other twenty-eight get a hold or a heading. Each instruction is a radio call and a readback, ten seconds each if nobody steps on anybody. Twenty-eight calls is five minutes, not four. Assume and label: five.

**Which aircraft cannot divert?**
I do not know. I ask. Each pilot takes ten to thirty seconds to answer a fuel question. Forty aircraft, one frequency. I cannot ask all of them.

**What does 19 minutes mean to you?**
Nothing yet. I see a big return south-west of the field. Nobody has told me where it will be in 19 minutes or what "outer marker" means in their model. That is not my call. Ask Titan Watch.

**What is the hardest decision you make in this window?**
Which eight of the twenty-eight get to land here and which twenty go somewhere else. Everyone wants to land here. I do not have the fuel picture to decide it.

**What would help?**
Fuel state next to every return on my scope, and the kaiju as a proper track with a time to the centreline, not a smear. I do not need a recommendation. I need to see.

## Coach observations

- Propagation is five minutes in role-play, not four. Conflict with the tower interview; logged, not resolved.
