# Scenario 1: Sixty-eight thousand at Lumen Field

**Slug:** `lumen-field-evac` - **Repo:** github.com/joelcapgemini/titan-watch-lab - **Your role:** incident commander, full authority, next page

## The kaiju, in thirty seconds

- **Gorathos.** Abyssal Colossus, 142 m, 88,000 t, 34.5 km/h. Walking ashore from Elliott Bay toward downtown through SoDo. Does not stop. Cannot be stopped. Anything it walks through is lost.
- **11 minutes** until it reaches the seawall. Straight-line estimate from Titan Watch, refreshed every 30 seconds. Firm on time, not on heading.
- **It is drawn toward dense noise and light.** The stadium is the loudest and brightest thing in the city. It does not pursue individuals.
- **Who sees it.** Titan Watch sees where it is, every 30 seconds. Stadium cameras see gates, police see streets, fans' phones see it first. Nobody sees which gate is safe. That gap is the scenario.

Sold out, 68,000 inside. Empty it into streets that face the kaiju, or hold them in a building never built to take a hit.

## Outcome, same for every scenario

Save as much human life as possible, then prevent as much public property damage as possible, in that order. A software tool is expected to come out of this.

**You are graded on, in this order:** people killed or injured inside and outside the stadium; crush injuries at gates; damage to Lumen Field and SoDo; and whether every decision you made was a decision, recorded, with a name on it.

The coach interviews you against this, narrows it to this clock and this building, and restates it after synthesis. The original stays in the scope file beside the restatement.
