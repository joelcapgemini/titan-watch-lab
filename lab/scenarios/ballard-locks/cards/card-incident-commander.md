# Your card: Incident commander, Ballard Locks

**Scenario 4, the locks.** Kaiju facts are on page 1 of this pack. Human facts are yours: invent and label.

**Outcome:** save as much human life as possible, then prevent as much public property damage as possible, in that order. The coach narrows it; that is fine.

**Slug:** `ballard-locks-alert-commander`

## Who you are

You are in charge. The Army Corps, Titan Watch, the Coast Guard, the county and three cities answer to you. You can close the locks, raise or hold an alert at any level in Seattle, Kirkland and Bellevue, and task sonar and the patrols. Nobody will argue. The only things you do not control are Skarnyx and the next return.

## Who you can call, and for what

- Lockmaster: gate strain, time and cost to close.
- Titan Watch analysts: the last return, the board.
- Seattle duty officer: the Seattle shore.
- Kirkland: the east shore, Bellevue by proxy.
- County alert officer: what an alert or a false one costs.
- Coast Guard: the Sound's surface, one boat.
- Biologist: could it be a whale.
- Harbour patrol: the lake side, the docks.

Each sees a piece. You see what they tell you, when they tell you. Your only screen is the Titan Watch command centre; everything else arrives by report, late.

## Your scope

In scope, you decide and order:
- Close the locks, yes or no, and when.
- Alert or hold, at what level, which cities, on what evidence.
- Where sonar and the patrols look next.

Out of scope:
- How a specialist carries out your order. They know their job.
- Where Skarnyx is or goes. Titan Watch reports it; nobody controls it.

## Anchors, fixed

- There is no clock. Nobody will tell you how long you have. Every wait costs; so does every alert.
- Every piece of evidence arrives through a different person on a different schedule, none with a confidence.

## Three phrases

"I do not know." - "Assume and label it." - "Ask the specialist, then tell me."
