# Assumptions log: i90-span-clear

Pre-seeded. Participants add to this through their own interviews; the coach will append under their slug. Every entry carries the consequence if wrong.

| ID | Assumption | Source | Consequence if wrong |
|----|-----------|--------|----------------------|
| A1 | Terrakon's track is straight-line from the current satellite position to the Mercer Island shoreline | Titan Watch model, world rules | The shore burst shortens the 26 minutes; any deadline built on it is late |
| A2 | "Anchor line" in the 26-minute figure means the island shoreline, not the bridge; the span is reached about 5 to 6 minutes earlier | Titan Watch analyst interview, role-play | If the figure already counts to the bridge, the margin is larger than feared; if to the shoreline, it is smaller |
| A3 | The east span from Mercer Island to Bellevue is open and untouched, so the island is rerouted, not stranded | Mercer Island interview, role-play; the emergency manager does not know | The island is cut off with no exit; the hard choice changes shape |
| A4 | A closure order reaches the road, signs and gates and ramps, within 4 to 5 minutes of being given | TMC and State Patrol interviews, role-play | The usable clearing window is shorter than it looks |
| A5 | Clearing the span, first action to confirmed empty, takes 15 to 20 minutes at commute load | TMC interview, role-play; never timed | The margin to T1 may already be negative when the order is given |
| A6 | T1, unsafe for traffic, arrives before Terrakon touches the span, because its wake loads the anchors first | Structural engineer interview, role-play | If T1 is at contact, the clock is longer; if earlier still, shorter |
| A7 | Light rail cannot divert or reverse on the span; a train committed to the crossing runs forward | World rules, rail control interview | None; frozen |
| A8 | There are about 1.5 people per vehicle, so about 6,000 people in the 4,000 vehicles, plus up to 800 on two trains | Facilitator | Changes the count, not the shape of the problem |

## Hypothesis

- H1: Software is part of the answer because nobody can compute T1 from Terrakon's track and the bridge's strain data, keep a countdown honest about its age, and hold one view of what is on the span, by voice in 20 minutes. Refuted if: a single existing system already holds the track, the strain data and the span contents; or the decision is purely one of authority.
