# Interview: driver, stopped mid-span

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Driver of a car with four people in it, eastbound, about halfway across the span in stopped commute traffic. Heading home to Mercer Island. Has the radio on. Has not been told anything.

## Can see

Brake lights ahead. The lane signal overhead, still green. The lake either side. A phone with a signal.

## Cannot see

Why the traffic has stopped. Terrakon; it is behind the hill and the trees. The sign at the entry, already passed. Anything the commander has decided.

## Questions and answers

**What do you know right now?**
Nothing. Traffic stopped two minutes ago. I assume a crash ahead. The radio is playing music. The signal over my lane is green, so as far as I can tell the road is open and slow.

**What would you do if the signal went red?**
Nothing different. Red means a lane is closing ahead, it does not mean get out. I would sit and wait to be merged. If you want me to leave the car and walk, somebody has to say that, in words, and say which way.

**Which way would you go?**
Forward. Home is east and I am past halfway. If you tell me to go back west I will argue unless you tell me why. Assume and label: half the drivers at mid-span will go the wrong way without a reason.

**What would make you abandon the car?**
A message on my phone that names the bridge and says leave the vehicle and walk east now. Or seeing it. If I see it, you have lost control of the span; everyone runs and nobody drives.

**What would help?**
One instruction, early, with a reason and a direction. "Hazard at the bridge, leave your vehicle, walk east, now." I do not need the countdown. I need to know which way, before the people behind me decide for me.

## Coach observations

- Silence is an instruction. A green signal says the road is open. Every driver not told otherwise will sit in the queue until the span goes.
- Direction matters as much as the order. Mid-span, half the drivers face the wrong way for the instruction they will get. The tool, if it reaches drivers at all, must say which way.
- "If I see it, you have lost control" sets a hard end to the usable window: the span must be empty before Terrakon is visible from it, not before it arrives.
