# Interview: Mercer Island emergency manager

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Emergency manager for the city of Mercer Island, about 25,000 residents. Two ways off: the I-90 span west to Seattle and the east span to Bellevue. Owns the island's response: fire, police, the hospital, two care homes, the schools. Is the declared target's representative and has not been called.

## Can see

The island. Local fire and police radio. The public Titan Watch alert, same as everyone. The news.

## Cannot see

Terrakon's track. The span. Whether the east span is threatened. Anything the commander has decided until it is public.

## Questions and answers

**How do you find out the span is closing?**
From the news, or from a resident who was turned back at the gate. Nobody from WSDOT or State Patrol has ever called this office about a closure. Assume and label: I learn ten minutes after the gates are down.

**What does stranding mean to you?**
Cut off without warning. Cut off is a Tuesday; we have one road east and it floods. Cut off with no call, while a kaiju walks toward us, with the hospital asking me what to do, is the thing I cannot recover from.

**Is the east span to Bellevue open?**
I do not know. Terrakon is on the Seattle side, so I assume it is, and I would route everyone east. But if it is also at risk, I have 25,000 people and no road. Nobody can tell me. Assume and label: open, unverified.

**Who cannot self-evacuate?**
The hospital, two care homes, the schools if it is daytime. Assume and label: 600 people who need a vehicle and a person. They need an hour I may not have, and they need it before the island is told to leave, not after, or the roads fill.

**What would you want?**
To be told what is happening and why, by name, before the gates drop. Not a threat level, not a siren. "The span closes at this time, Terrakon reaches your shore at this time, the east span is open." Three facts. I can run the island from three facts.

## Coach observations

- The island learns last and from the news. Q6 is open on channel, timing and content; the manager has supplied the content.
- The east span (A3) is the hinge of the hard choice and nobody knows its status. Flag it to the commander as the first question to ask the Titan Watch liaison.
- "Three facts, by name, before the gates drop" is the island-notification requirement, stated by the person who would receive it. It ends the tool's scope at Q3.
