# Interview: tow and recovery supervisor, I-90 contract

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Supervisor for the contract tow operator that covers the span. Four trucks on a commute, staged at either end. Clears stalled and crashed vehicles so the lanes move. Reports to the TMC when called, otherwise works from the dispatch queue.

## Can see

Where the four trucks are. The dispatch queue of reported stalls. The lanes, from a truck cab, once a truck is on the span.

## Cannot see

Stalls nobody has reported. Terrakon. The countdown. Camera feeds; those are the TMC's. Whether the span is being cleared or just slow.

## Questions and answers

**On an ordinary commute, how many vehicles on the span will not move?**
Three or four at any time: a stall, a flat, a fender-bender. Assume and label: four. Each one in a live lane halves the drain rate for everything behind it. A stall nobody has reported is a stall I do not know about until the queue behind it is a kilometre long.

**How long to reach one mid-span?**
Ten minutes from the end against a full span, because I am driving the shoulder past stopped traffic. Assume and label: ten. On a closing span I am driving toward the thing everyone else is driving away from, and my drivers will ask why.

**What happens to a stuck vehicle when the span is cleared?**
It stays. If I cannot reach it in time the people in it have to walk. Nobody tells them that today; they sit with the hazards on and wait for me.

**How do you find out the span is closing?**
The TMC calls, if they remember. I am not on the list for the Titan Watch alert, and I have never seen a WSDOT closure order that mentioned tow. I usually find out when the queue empties behind the gate.

**What would you want?**
The stalls on one list with the TMC's, with a position, so I send the nearest truck and not the first one. And the countdown, so I can tell a driver "you have eight minutes, go, or walk". I will not send a truck onto the span inside five minutes of that number.

## Coach observations

- Four stuck vehicles halve the drain rate and are invisible until reported. The 15 to 20 minute clearing estimate assumes nothing is stuck; it probably is.
- Tow holds the only knowledge of which vehicles will not move and is not on any distribution. Q5 has a third owner now: TMC, rail control, tow.
- "I will not send a truck inside five minutes" is a specialist's own cut-off against the countdown. The tool must be good enough for people to set their own limits on it.
