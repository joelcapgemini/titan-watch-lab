# Stakeholder map: i90-span-clear

Pre-seeded. Nine roles marked DONE have role-play interviews in this folder. The participant plays the incident commander, marked INTERVIEW.

| Stakeholder | Feels the problem how | Controls | Status |
|-------------|----------------------|----------|--------|
| People in the 4,000 vehicles and on the trains | Most directly, least information | Their own vehicle only | One driver interviewed; the rest voiced through others |
| WSDOT traffic management centre operator | Physically closes the span, cannot count what is on it | Signs, lane signals, gates, cameras | DONE |
| WSDOT structural engineer | Knows when the span is unsafe, cannot see why | Strain and tilt tolerances, the T1 call | DONE |
| State Patrol ground commander | Blocks the ramps, cannot reach anyone already on the span | Four units, ramp closures | DONE |
| Mercer Island emergency manager | 25,000 people on the far side, learns from the news | Island response, nothing on the span | DONE |
| Sound Transit light-rail control | 400 people per train, cannot divert, cannot reverse mid-span | Train holds at the stations either side | DONE |
| Titan Watch duty analyst | Produces the 26-minute figure | Satellite track, arrival model | DONE |
| Titan Watch field observer | Sees what Terrakon is doing, not where it is | One radio to the analyst | DONE |
| A driver mid-span | One car, four people, no information | One car | DONE |
| Tow and recovery supervisor | Knows which vehicles will not move | Four trucks, where they go | DONE |
| Incident commander | Full authority, sees only reports | Everything: closure, trains, ramps, island warning, the Titan Watch liaison | INTERVIEW, the participant |
| King County alerting, WEA and EAS | Only channel to people not on the span | Public alert text and timing | Assumed |
| Bellevue and Seattle fire and EMS | Receive whatever goes wrong at either end | Their own units | Assumed |
| Coast Guard, vessels on the lake | Under and beside the span | Marine traffic | Assumed |
| Public and media | Learn of it when the gates drop | Nothing | Assumed |

## Who decides, who acts, who is told

- Decides: the incident commander, full authority by scenario rule.
- Acts: TMC (signs, signals, gates), State Patrol (ramps), rail control (train holds), tow and recovery (stuck vehicles), structural engineer (the T1 call).
- Told: drivers, Mercer Island, Bellevue, the public.

The gap the coach will probably find: the person who knows when the span is unsafe (structural engineer) and the person who knows where Terrakon is (Titan Watch) have no channel to each other, so nobody computes T1; and the people who know what is on the span (TMC, rail control, tow) each hold a piece and nobody holds the whole.
