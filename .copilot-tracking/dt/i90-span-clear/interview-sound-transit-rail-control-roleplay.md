# Interview: Sound Transit light-rail control

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Controller at the light-rail operations centre. Runs the trains across the I-90 span. Can hold a train at the station on either side. Cannot do anything to a train once it is on the span except talk to the operator.

## Can see

Every train's position on the line, to the block. Passenger loading estimates from the stations. Radio to every operator. Phone from the TMC.

## Cannot see

Terrakon. The countdown. Road traffic on the span. Anything from Titan Watch except the public alert.

## Questions and answers

**How many people are on the span by rail right now?**
Commute load, two trains could be on it at once, one each way. About 400 people per train. Assume and label: 800 at worst, zero if I am lucky with the timing.

**Can a train divert or reverse?**
No. The line is fixed and a train on the span runs forward to the far station. The crossing takes about three minutes. Assume and label: three. Once I have released a train from the station on either side, it is committed. The only control I have is the release.

**When do you need the hold order?**
Before the last train I would otherwise release. If the span must be empty at a time, my hold has to come three minutes before that, plus the time it takes you to tell me. I would rather hold early and apologise. Today the TMC calls me after they have started closing, which is too late for a train already released.

**How do you find out?**
A phone call from the TMC, usually one of many they are making. I am not on the Titan Watch distribution. I would hear it on the news like the passengers.

**What would you want?**
The same countdown the TMC sees, on my board, so I can work out my own last release time instead of waiting for a call. And my trains on the span to count as people in whatever list the commander is reading. Today 800 people are invisible to the road side.

## Coach observations

- Rail holds have to happen before the road closure, not with it. The order of operations in the chain is wrong by default.
- 800 people on two trains are not in the TMC's count. Q5 is confirmed from the rail side: the span contents live in at least two systems that do not meet.
- The second request for the same countdown on an existing board. Integration into displays people already watch is a constraint, as in the Sea-Tac scenario.
