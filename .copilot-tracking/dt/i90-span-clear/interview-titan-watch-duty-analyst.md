# Interview: Titan Watch duty analyst

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Duty analyst at the Project Titan Watch command centre. Produces the track and the 26-minute figure. Has no road authority, no bridge training, and has never spoken to anyone at WSDOT.

## Can see

The satellite track: position, heading, speed for Terrakon every 30 seconds. The threat board. The field observers' radio reports.

## Cannot see

The bridge. Its strain, its traffic, its tolerances. Whether anyone at WSDOT is reading the board.

## Questions and answers

**Where does 26 minutes come from?**
Straight-line distance from the last satellite fix to the Mercer Island shoreline, divided by current speed, 22.5 km/h. The shoreline is the declared target. We call the point the anchor line because the target marker sits on the shore. It is not the bridge.

**So when does it reach the bridge?**
Nobody has computed that. The bridge is not a target in our model; it is in the way. On this heading it crosses the span line before the shoreline. Assume and label: five to six minutes before, so about 20, not 26.

**How much does the figure move?**
Every fix. It is straight-line and Terrakon accelerates near shore, so the misses are early, not late. Assume and label: 20 percent early is typical. Satellite fixes also drop when cloud or spray covers it, and the figure goes stale without saying so; it just stops changing.

**Who do you tell?**
The threat board and the citywide threat condition. There is no line to WSDOT. They would learn from the public alert or the news. Whether anyone there watches the board, I do not know.

**What would you want to give the bridge?**
Not a number. A range, with the early edge marked, the age of the last fix beside it, and a flag when fixes stop. And someone on their side who can turn it into a bridge time, because I cannot.

## Coach observations

- The 26 minutes is the island, not the bridge. Q2 is answered in role-play, and the answer shortens the clock to about 20 before the early bias is applied. Plan for 16.
- Staleness is silent. A countdown that stops changing looks like a countdown that is holding steady. The age of the last fix is as important as the figure.
- Titan Watch cannot compute a bridge time and WSDOT cannot see the track. Q4 is confirmed from the second side: the join is missing, not the data.
