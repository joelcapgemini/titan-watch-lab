# Interview: Titan Watch duty analyst

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Duty analyst at the Project Titan Watch command centre. Produces the track estimate and the 19-minute figure. Has no aviation authority and no aviation training.

## Can see

The sensor grid: position, heading, speed for Vespyra every 30 seconds. The threat board. Public weather.

## Cannot see

Aircraft. Fuel. Runways. Whether anyone at Sea-Tac is reading the threat board.

## Questions and answers

**Where does 19 minutes come from?**
Distance from the last ping to the point where Vespyra's projected track crosses the extended centreline of the 16 runways, divided by current speed. Straight line. We call that point the outer marker because someone on the aviation side used the phrase once. I do not know if it matches the real outer marker.

**How much does it move?**
Every ping. Vespyra accelerates into weather. There is a front south-west of the field. If it rides that, 19 becomes 14. I cannot say when.

**What do you tell, and to whom?**
We raise the threat condition on the board and send a citywide alert when it crosses a threshold. Sea-Tac has a liaison who watches the board. Whether the liaison can reach the tower in time, I do not know. We have no direct line to the FAA.

**Has the estimate been wrong?**
Yes. Early, more often than late. It arrives sooner than straight-line because of the weather bursts. Assume and label: 20 percent early is typical.

**What would you want to give Sea-Tac?**
Not a number. A range, with the early edge marked, updated every ping, and a flag when a ping is missed so nobody trusts a stale one.

## Coach observations

- "Outer marker" is a borrowed phrase. Q2 is now a definition problem on both sides.
- The estimate runs early by about 20 percent in role-play. Any deadline built on 19 minutes should plan for 15.
- Titan Watch has no line to the FAA. Same organisational gap as the tower interview, from the other side.
- The analyst asked for a range with the early edge marked and a staleness flag. That is a requirement, if it survives the other interviews.
