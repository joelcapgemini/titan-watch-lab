# World rules: Scenario 3, forty aircraft on approach

Fixed facts for this scenario. Do not invent monster facts. Human facts (how your organisation works, what you would do, what breaks) are yours to invent and label.

## The kaiju

| Fact | Value | Source |

| Archetype | Tempest Wyrm, airborne | `data/` roster |
| Size | 98 m, 41,000 t | `data/` roster |
| Speed | 62 km/h sustained, bursts higher near weather | `data/` roster; burst is lore |
| Declared target | Bellevue | `data/` roster |
| Current status | Critical, inbound | `data/` roster |
| Path | Crosses the Sea-Tac approach corridor from the south-west, never changes heading for aircraft | Lore |

## Behaviour

- Vespyra rides storm fronts. It accelerates into weather and slows in clear air. Nobody in this scenario can predict weather better than the public forecast.
- It does not attack aircraft deliberately. Aircraft in its path are hit by wake and debris. Anything within about 2 km of its track is lost.
- It is visible on primary radar as a slow, large, erratic return. Secondary radar (transponder) shows nothing. Titan Watch's sensor grid tracks it separately.
- It does not land, feed or stop in this scenario. It transits.

## The clock

- 19 minutes from the scenario start until Vespyra crosses the outer marker for runways 16L, 16C and 16R.
- "Outer marker" is Titan Watch's phrase. Nobody has yet confirmed whether that is the point where aircraft on final are at risk, or the point where the field itself is.
- Titan Watch updates its track estimate every 30 seconds. The estimate is straight-line and does not model weather bursts.

## Sensing, by source

| Source | Sees | Does not see | Cadence |
|--------|------|--------------|---------|
| Titan Watch sensor grid | Position, heading, speed | Intent, weather response | Every 30 s |
| ATC primary radar | A large erratic return | What it is, its altitude with confidence | Continuous |
| Pilots | Visual contact inside about 15 km in daylight | Anything in cloud | On report |
| Public weather feed | Storm fronts | Vespyra | Every 10 min |

No single source sees both where Vespyra is and what it is about to do.

## Hard choice

Land everything onto runways in its path, or divert forty aircraft with fuel some of them do not have.

## Not in this scenario

- Weapons, interception, engagement of Vespyra.
- Passenger terminal evacuation.
- Anything after Vespyra has passed.
