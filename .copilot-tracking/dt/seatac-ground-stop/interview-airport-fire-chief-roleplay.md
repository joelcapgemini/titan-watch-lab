# Interview: airport fire and rescue chief, Port of Seattle

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Chief of aircraft rescue and firefighting. Positions crews and vehicles for anything that goes wrong on landing. Reports to the airport duty manager.

## Can see

The runways and taxiways from the fire stations. The tower's crash alert line. Radio with the tower and the duty manager.

## Cannot see

Aircraft in the air. Fuel state. Vespyra. Anything on the approach frequency.

## Questions and answers

**What changes for you when thirty aircraft land in fifteen minutes instead of forty in an hour?**
Everything. We stage for one emergency at a time. Thirty landings back to back means a runway excursion on 16L while something else is still rolling on 16C. I have five vehicles. I can cover two runways properly or three badly. Assume and label: two.

**What do you need to know before the first one lands?**
Which runways are in use and in what order. Whether anyone is landing heavy with fuel, because a full aircraft landing fast is a different fire. The tower tells me runway. Nobody tells me fuel.

**What happens if Vespyra crosses the field while you have crews on the runway?**
I pull them. I do not know how much warning I would get. I hear about Titan Watch from the duty manager, who hears from somewhere else. By the time it reaches me it has been through three people.

**Has anything like this been drilled?**
Mass diversion, once, for a volcanic ash event in the plan. Never against a clock. The drill took two hours of landings and we still had a vehicle in the wrong place.

**What would you want from a tool?**
A countdown I can trust and a list of what is landing, in order, with anything unusual flagged. Not a map. I cannot read a map and drive.

## Coach observations

- Warning reaches fire and rescue through three people. The decision-to-action chain has a known lag at the end as well as the start.
- "Not a map" is a design input. The command map in the sample app is the wrong artefact for this user.
- Fuel is unknown to the tower and to fire and rescue. The same gap appears in every interview so far.
