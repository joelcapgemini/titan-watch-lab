# Interview: Army Corps lockmaster, Ballard Locks

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Lockmaster on duty. Operates the large and small lock chambers and the spillway gates. Owns the gate sensors. Reports to the Army Corps district, not to the city. Has never closed the locks for anything but maintenance and flood.

## Can see

Gate position, chamber water level, strain on the gate leaves, salt intrusion readings at the gate. Vessels waiting on both sides. The surface of the channel from the control tower.

## Cannot see

Anything under the surface outside the chamber. Sonar. Skarnyx or a whale, until it is in the channel. Whether anyone at Titan Watch is watching the gates.

## Questions and answers

**What happens at the locks if something 88 m long tries to come through?**
The gates are built for water pressure, not for being pushed. If it leans on the large lock gate the leaf gives. Then the canal runs out to the Sound and the lake drops. Assume and label: the lake loses a metre in the first hour. I do not know how fast the salt comes back in behind it.

**Can you close the locks now?**
Yes. Both chambers shut and the spillway down takes about twenty minutes with the crew I have, and I have to clear every vessel out of the chambers first. Nobody has asked me to. I would do it on a phone call from someone I recognise. Assume and label: twenty minutes, forty if there is a vessel mid-cycle.

**What does closing cost?**
Every vessel waiting goes nowhere. Fishing boats miss a tide, tour boats cancel, a few barges. Money, and complaints to the district. It does not cost anyone their life unless a vessel is stuck in the chamber when the gate goes. I would want that written down somewhere, because right now it is my call to make and my name on it.

**What would your sensors tell you?**
Strain on the gate leaves goes up before anything breaks. Salt reading at the gate climbs if the seal is lost. I see it on my panel. Nobody else does. There is no feed out of this tower.

**What do you need that you do not have?**
Someone to tell me how sure they are. If Titan Watch says there is a return in Shilshole Bay, I need to know if that is a seal or a serpent before I shut down the canal for a day. Nobody puts a number on it.

## Coach observations

- Closing the gates is a cheaper first step than a three-city alert and nobody has asked for it. The hard choice on the scenario card is missing a third option.
- The lock sensors are a second evidence source and they stay in the tower. Nobody fuses them with sonar.
- "Nobody puts a number on it" is the confidence gap, seen from the person who would act first.
