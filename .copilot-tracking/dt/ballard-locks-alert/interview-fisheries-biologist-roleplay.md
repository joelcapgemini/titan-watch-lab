# Interview: fisheries biologist, fish ladder and marine mammal programme

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Biologist who works the fish ladder at the locks and keeps the sea-lion and whale sighting records for the area. Knows what normally swims into Shilshole Bay and when. Nobody has called them.

## Can see

Sighting records for years back. The sea-lion haul-out at the locks. The whale-watch network's reports. The salmon run calendar.

## Cannot see

Sonar. The threat board. Anything Titan Watch has. That anyone is worried, until this interview.

## Questions and answers

**What is normally in the water off the locks right now?**
Sea lions, dozens, this time of year. Harbour seals. Grey whales come into the Sound in spring, less so now. Assume and label: one grey whale sighting in the last month, in the wrong place for this. Nothing 88 m long. Nothing close to it.

**Could a sonar return off Shilshole be a whale?**
A single return, yes. A string of them moving east toward the locks, no. Whales do not go for the locks. Sea lions do, for the salmon, and they are small. If the return is big and heading for fresh water, that is not anything I have a record of.

**Has anyone asked you?**
No. The whale-watch network would tell me within the hour if something was on the surface. It has not. That is evidence too, and nobody is collecting it.

**What would you look for at the locks?**
The sea lions leave. They are loud and they are always there and if they go quiet and go, something they do not like is in the water. I would put that above any sonar return.

**What would you want?**
Someone to ask. I could rule a whale in or out in ten minutes from my records. Put me on whatever page the sonar is on and I will annotate it.

## Coach observations

- The evidence to rule out the false alarm exists and nobody has asked for it. The sonar analyst said "someone who knows whales"; here they are.
- Absence of a surface sighting from the whale-watch network is itself evidence. The sources that lower confidence are as unrecorded as the ones that raise it.
- The sea lions leaving is a cheap, local, non-technical sensor. It will not survive a PRD unless someone writes it down.
