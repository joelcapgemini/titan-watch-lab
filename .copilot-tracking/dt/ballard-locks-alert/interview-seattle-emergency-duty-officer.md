# Interview: Seattle emergency management duty officer

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Duty officer at the Seattle emergency operations centre. First city to hear, because Titan Watch's declared target is Seattle. Owns the Seattle shoreline of the Ship Canal, Lake Union and the western lake shore. Requests alerts from the county; does not send them.

## Can see

The Titan Watch board. Phone traffic from harbour patrol, police and fire. The county alert request form. The other two cities, by phone.

## Cannot see

Sonar. Lock sensors. Whether Kirkland and Bellevue have been told the same thing at the same time. Whether the return is real.

## Questions and answers

**What have you done so far?**
Opened the EOC at a low level. Called harbour patrol and asked them to look. Called the county and asked what the alert would take. Called Kirkland. Not Bellevue yet. Assume and label: forty minutes since the first return and four phone calls.

**What would make you ask for the alert?**
Something I could defend afterwards. A surface sighting with a photograph. The lockmaster saying strain is up. Two sources that agree. One sonar return from Titan Watch is not something I can put three cities on the move for. I have said that to Titan Watch. They have no answer because they have nothing else to give me.

**What does a false alarm cost you?**
The waterfront empties, the news runs it, and the next time we send, half of them stay home. Nobody has written down a number. Assume and label: the next alert loses half its compliance.

**Can the gates be closed instead?**
I have not asked. It is not mine to ask; it is the Army Corps. I would want the commander to ask. It would buy time and it would not frighten anyone.

**What do you need that you do not have?**
Everyone's evidence on one page, with how old it is and how sure they are, and one decision line under it that says what we are doing and who said so. Today I have four phone calls in a notebook and three cities asking me what Seattle is doing.

## Coach observations

- "Two sources that agree" is a working evidence threshold the duty officer has set for themselves. Nobody has written it down and the other two cities may have a different one.
- Seattle hears first and Bellevue has not been called. The three cities are already out of step before any decision is made.
- The duty officer wants gates closed but will not ask. The commander has the authority; Q1 is lag and confidence, not ownership.
