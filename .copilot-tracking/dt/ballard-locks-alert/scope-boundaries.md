# Scope boundaries: ballard-locks-alert

Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1a complete. Participants do not redo this; they add interviews under their own slug and the lead synthesises.

## Problem statement, as currently understood

Skarnyx is somewhere in Puget Sound, submerged, and the only evidence is an intermittent sonar log. If it passes the Ballard Locks the lakes are lost. Three cities share the lake shoreline. Nobody today has, in one place, what the sonar shows, what the lock sensors show, what has been seen on the surface, and how much to trust any of it. The commander must decide whether to alert three cities on that, or wait.

## Outcome

Starting outcome, fixed for every scenario: Save as much human life as possible, then prevent as much public property damage as possible, in that order. A software tool is expected to come out of this.

## In scope

- The window from the first submerged return to either a confirmed sighting or a breach of the gates. There is no clock; the window is as long as the evidence stays ambiguous.
- The decision chain from a sonar return to an alert in three cities, or to a decision not to alert.
- The information each decider needs and does not have, and how confident they are in what they do have.
- A software tool is expected to come out of this. Which part of the chain it serves is open.

## Out of scope

- Engaging or deflecting Skarnyx.
- Evacuation beyond the three waterfronts.
- Recovery after a breach.
- Anything that assumes Skarnyx surfaces on request.

## Frozen and fluid

| Constraint | Frozen or fluid | Note |
|------------|-----------------|------|
| No clock | Frozen | World rule |
| Three cities on the lake shoreline | Frozen | Scenario card |
| Sonar return as sufficient evidence | Fluid | Open question Q2 |
| Closing the gates as a step short of an alert | Fluid | Lockmaster interview; cost unknown |
| Who decides | Frozen | The incident commander, full authority, by scenario rule |
| Software as part of the answer | Fluid | Hypothesis H1, to be tested |

## Boundary questions to revisit

- Q1: How long does an order take to reach the people who act, and a piece of evidence to reach the commander? The commander has full authority; the gap is lag and confidence, not ownership.
- Q2: What counts as enough evidence to alert three cities, and who has written that down?
- Q3: What does the tool stop caring about once the gates are closed, or once Skarnyx surfaces?
