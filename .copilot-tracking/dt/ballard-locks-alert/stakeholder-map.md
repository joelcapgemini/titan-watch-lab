## Who decides, who acts, who is told

- Decides: the incident commander, full authority by scenario rule.
- Acts: lockmaster (gates), county alert officer (send), Coast Guard and harbour patrol (surface search), Titan Watch (sonar tasking).
- Told: the three city duty officers, residents, vessel traffic, public.

The gap the coach will probably find: the people who hold evidence (sonar, lock sensors, surface, biology) each hold one kind and none can put a confidence on it, and the person who sends the alert has one button with no level on it.
