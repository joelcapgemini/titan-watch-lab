# Interview: King County alert and warning officer

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Alert and warning officer at the King County emergency coordination centre. Sends wireless emergency alerts to phones inside a drawn polygon. Sends on request from a city, with a template and a sign-off. Has sent for floods, fires and one missing child. Never for something that might not be there.

## Can see

The alert console, the polygon tool, the templates, who asked and when. Delivery reports after a send.

## Cannot see

Sonar. The locks. Whether the thing is real. Whether the three cities agree.

## Questions and answers

**What happens when a city asks for an alert?**
They give me a reason, an area and a message. I check it against the template, get a sign-off, draw the polygon and send. Ten minutes if the request is clean. Thirty if three cities want three different messages and I have to make it one. Assume and label: ten clean, thirty messy.

**Is there a level below a full alert?**
On the phone system, no. It goes to every phone in the polygon at full volume or it does not go. There are other channels, social media, sirens where there are sirens, but those are the cities'. The thing I own is all or nothing.

**Can you send to one city and not the others?**
I can draw any polygon. Whether I should send to the Seattle shore and not Kirkland when they share a lake is a question nobody has answered. Assume and label: I would refuse without all three asking, or the commander ordering.

**What does a false alarm cost?**
Opt-outs. Every time we send something that turns out to be nothing, people turn the alerts off on their phones, and they do not turn them back on. We do not have a number. Assume and label: a few percent per false send, permanent.

**What would you want?**
A reason I can quote in the message that is more than "sonar return". And a decision record that says who decided, on what, so that when it is a whale I can show it was not me guessing.

## Coach observations

- The wireless alert is all or nothing by design. A graded response has to come from somewhere else and nobody has built it.
- Opt-outs are permanent in role-play. The cost of a false alarm compounds across every future scenario, not just this one.
- The county wants a decision record with a name on it. That is the fifth grading item and the county is asking for it unprompted.
