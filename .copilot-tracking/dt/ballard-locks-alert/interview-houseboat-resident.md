# Interview: houseboat resident, Lake Union

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Lives on a floating home on the east side of Lake Union, a few hundred metres of water from the Ship Canal. Has lived there twenty years. Knows the lake level by the gap between the dock and the walkway. Has heard nothing official.

## Can see

The lake, the canal mouth, the harbour patrol boat when it passes. A phone with alerts turned on. Neighbours.

## Cannot see

Sonar. The locks. Anything on the Sound. Why the harbour patrol went past twice in an hour.

## Questions and answers

**What do you know right now?**
Nothing. The patrol boat went by twice, which is unusual, and a neighbour said something about Titan Watch. I checked the board on my phone and it says submerged. I do not know what I am supposed to do with "submerged".

**What would you do if the alert went off?**
Leave. The dock is the only way out and it floats. If the lake drops a metre, the ramp tilts and the gas line to the shore pulls. Assume and label: ten minutes to get off if the ramp holds, longer if everyone goes at once.

**What would you do if it went off and nothing happened?**
Come back. Then next time, wait and see. Everyone on this dock would. We have had the tsunami test and the flood warnings and nothing ever comes. One more and the alert is just noise.

**What would you want to be told, short of an alert?**
That the locks are closed. That I would believe, because it is a thing someone did, not a thing someone thinks. Tell me the gates are shut and the patrol is on the lake and I will keep my shoes by the door.

**What do you need?**
A reason. "Sonar return" means nothing to me. "Gates closed, patrol on the water, come off the dock if the lake moves" is something I can act on.

## Coach observations

- The resident would believe a closed gate before an alert. An action is more credible than a warning, and it costs less.
- Each false alarm converts a resident from "leave" to "wait and see". Assumption A6, half compliance per false alarm, is carried here in plain words.
- "Come off the dock if the lake moves" is a trigger the resident can see for themselves. The lake level is a sensor the public already has.
