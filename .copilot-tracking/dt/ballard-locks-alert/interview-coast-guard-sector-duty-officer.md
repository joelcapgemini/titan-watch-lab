# Interview: Coast Guard sector duty officer, Puget Sound

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Sector duty officer. Owns vessel traffic on the Sound and the approach to the locks. Can divert shipping, close a channel, put a boat on the water. Owns the surface. Does not own what is under it.

## Can see

Vessel traffic on the Sound. Radio with every commercial vessel. One patrol boat near Shilshole. Weather and sea state.

## Cannot see

Below the surface. Sonar, unless Titan Watch sends it. Lock sensors. The lake side, which is harbour patrol's.

## Questions and answers

**What have you done?**
Put the patrol boat off Shilshole and told it to look for anything. Warned shipping of an unconfirmed hazard. That is all I can do with what I have. Assume and label: one boat, two crew, daylight for four more hours.

**What has the boat seen?**
Disturbed water, once, that could have been a tide line. The coxswain phoned it to Titan Watch and to me. He did not take a photograph. I have told him to next time.

**Can you confirm a sonar return?**
No. I can tell you nothing is on the surface. That is not the same as nothing being there. I would want a sonar analyst on my radio telling me where to look, and I do not have one.

**What would you do if the commander ordered the channel closed?**
Close it. Stop vessels entering the locks approach. Hour to clear what is already there. It does not stop the serpent, it stops boats being in the way of it.

**What do you need?**
One picture with the sonar return, my boat, the lockmaster's gate and whatever the harbour patrol has, so that when my coxswain says disturbed water someone can put it next to the return and say "that is where it was". Today those are four radios.

## Coach observations

- The surface search is one boat with no photograph and no sonar on the radio. Surface sightings cannot be matched to returns because nobody holds both.
- The Coast Guard is willing to close the channel on an order. Like the lockmaster, a cheaper step exists and nobody has asked.
- "Four radios" is the fusion gap, stated as hardware.
