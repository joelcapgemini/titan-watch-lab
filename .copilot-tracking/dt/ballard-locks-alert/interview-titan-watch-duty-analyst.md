# Interview: Titan Watch duty analyst

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Duty analyst at the Project Titan Watch command centre. Holds the threat board and raises the threat condition. For Vespyra there was a clock on the board. For Skarnyx there is a status line that says "sensor grid only, submerged" and nothing else.

## Can see

The threat board, the roster, the sonar analyst's log. Whether the threat condition has been raised. The public alert channel.

## Cannot see

Lock sensors. Surface sightings unless someone phones them in. What a return means. Whether the three cities have anyone watching the board.

## Questions and answers

**What is on the board for Skarnyx?**
Codename, archetype, size, declared target Seattle, status submerged. No time to anything. The board was built for things that move toward a landmark. This one is sitting under the water somewhere and the board has nowhere to put that.

**When do you raise the threat condition?**
When it crosses a threshold. The threshold is written for surfaced kaiju: confirmed position, heading, speed. Skarnyx has none of those. So by the rule, never. By judgement, I do not know. Assume and label: no written threshold for a submerged kaiju.

**Who do you tell?**
The board goes to the three city duty officers and the county. Same page for all of them. I cannot send Seattle a different level from Kirkland. One condition, three cities.

**What came in from outside?**
A harbour patrol boat phoned about disturbed water off Shilshole. It went into a notebook. The lockmaster has not called. Nobody has asked me about whales.

**What would you want?**
A board that can hold "something, somewhere, this confident" and update when a sighting or a sensor reading comes in, from anyone, with who said it and when. Right now the only evidence I can show is the sonar log and that is one column.

## Coach observations

- The threat board has no row for a submerged, stationary kaiju. The tool that exists was designed for scenarios 1 to 3.
- A phone call about disturbed water went into a notebook. Surface sightings are the third evidence source and they are the least recorded.
- "One condition, three cities" is the graded-alert gap, seen from the sender's side before the county even gets it.
