# Interview: section steward, 300 level west concourse

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Event steward for sections 330 to 334 on the upper west concourse. Four hundred people, one stair to the 200 level, one radio on a channel shared with forty stewards. Nearest gate is West 2, which opens toward the water. Has heard "stand by" and nothing else.

## Can see

Four hundred people, the stair, the West 2 queue two levels down, and the water from the concourse rail. Phones lighting up in every row.

## Cannot see

The clock. Which gates are held. What the PA is about to say. Where the east gates are from here without the signs, and the signs point to West 2.

## Questions and answers

**What do you know right now?**
The radio said stand by. The people in 332 have a video of it coming out of the bay and they are already standing. I have no number, no direction and no reason to give them. Assume and label: a third of my section is already on its feet.

**What would you do if told to hold?**
Say hold, and put myself at the top of the stair. That works for thirty seconds. After that they go round me, because they have seen the video and I have given them nothing but a word. I will not stand in front of four hundred people with a word.

**What would you do if told nothing?**
They leave. They go down the stair to West 2 because that is the way they came in and the signs say exit. Silence means the nearest gate, and the nearest gate is the wrong one.

**How many stewards on this channel will do the same?**
All of us. Forty stewards, forty sections, one channel. If the plan is to send the west side east, somebody has to say so, to each of us, with the gate name, in time to walk it.

**What would help?**
One instruction, early, with a gate name and a reason I can say out loud. "Sections 330 to 334, down to the 200 level, East 4, it is coming from the water" is enough. I do not need the picture. I need the call, and I need it before the phones give them a different one.

## Coach observations

- Silence is an instruction. Every section not told otherwise walks to the nearest gate, and on the west side the nearest gate faces the track. Doing nothing is a decision with a known outcome.
- Fans will self-evacuate after a short wait. The window to coordinate is shorter than the clock; it is the crowd's patience, set by their phones.
- "I need the call, not the picture" is the sharpest version of the display rejection in the other interviews, and it adds a gate name per section to the gate board.
- The steward has no way to tell the operations centre what is happening on the stair. The report path is as thin as the order path.
