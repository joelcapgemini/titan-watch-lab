# Assumptions log: lumen-field-evac

Pre-seeded. Participants add to this through their own interviews; the coach will append under their slug. Every entry carries the consequence if wrong.

| ID | Assumption | Source | Consequence if wrong |
|----|-----------|--------|----------------------|
| A1 | Gorathos's track is straight-line from the last sensor position to the seawall and then to downtown | Titan Watch model, world rules | If it bends toward the stadium's noise and light, the stadium is on the track and the 13-minute figure is late |
| A2 | "Seawall" is when the stadium is at risk, not two minutes later when Gorathos reaches the stadium | Facilitator | If the stadium is the risk point, there are two more minutes to use; if the corridor is wider than assumed, east gates face it too |

| A7 | Nothing stops Gorathos inside the clock | World rules | None; frozen |
| A8 | A dark, quiet stadium is less attractive to Gorathos than a lit, loud one | World rules, lore, untested | If false, going dark costs the PA and the lights the crowd needs to move, for nothing |
