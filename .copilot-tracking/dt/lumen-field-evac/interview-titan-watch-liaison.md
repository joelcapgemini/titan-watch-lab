# Interview: Titan Watch liaison at Lumen Field

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Titan Watch's person in the stadium operations centre on event night. Watches the threat board on a laptop. Has no stadium authority and no radio on any stadium channel.

## Can see

The Titan Watch threat board, refreshed every 30 seconds. The operations director across the room. The CCTV wall, over the director's shoulder.

## Cannot see

Gates. Streets. Which way a gate faces. Whether anyone on a concourse has heard. Authority.

## Questions and answers

**What do you do when the board says 11 minutes?**
I walk to the director and say it. Then I go back to the laptop. If it changes, I walk again. The director turns and tells the gate supervisor on the radio, the gate supervisor tells the crews, the crews tell the stewards. Assume and label: thirty seconds per walk, and I miss a refresh each time. By the time a steward hears a number it has been through four people.

**Do you trust 11?**
For the seawall, yes. For the stadium, no. The board shows the heading bending toward us a little every ping. I would tell anyone who asked to plan for the stadium being on the track. Nobody has asked.

**What does "seawall" mean?**
Where the water stops. I asked the command centre once and they said it is the projected track crossing Alaskan Way. The director may hear it as "when the stadium is hit". That is two minutes of difference and nobody has reconciled it. That is not my call.

**Can you tell the gate supervisor directly?**
I have no radio and no standing to give them anything. If I walked to a gate and said hold it, they would ask who I was.

**What would you want?**
To put the board's two numbers, with the bend and their age, on whatever the director and the gate supervisor already look at. Not a new screen. Theirs.

## Coach observations

- The clock reaches the concourses by a person walking across a room and three radio calls. Four people, no age on the number at any step.
- The liaison would say "the stadium is on the track" if asked. Nobody asks. The analyst's bend warning is confirmed from the receiving side.
- "Seawall" means two different things in the same room, two minutes apart. Q2 is live in the operations centre tonight.
- "Not a new screen, theirs" is the fourth interview to reject a new display. Integration into the gate board is a constraint, not a preference.
