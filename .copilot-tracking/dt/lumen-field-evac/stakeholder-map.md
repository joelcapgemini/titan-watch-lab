# Stakeholder map: lumen-field-evac

Pre-seeded. Nine roles marked DONE have role-play interviews in this folder. The participant plays the incident commander, marked INTERVIEW.

| Stakeholder | Feels the problem how | Controls | Status |
|-------------|----------------------|----------|--------|
| 68,000 fans in the bowl | Most directly, least information, phones faster than any channel | Their own feet | Not interviewed; voiced through the steward |
| Stadium operations director | Owns the building, the lights, the PA, every steward | Gate policy, lights, PA, hold or release | DONE |
| Gate and crowd-flow supervisor | Opens and closes gates by radio, no record | Each gate, one at a time | DONE |

| Titan Watch duty analyst | Produces the 11-minute estimate | Sensor grid, track model | DONE |

| Section steward, 300 level west | 400 people, one radio, told nothing | One section, one stair | DONE |

| City emergency management | Owns the citywide alert | The public alert, slowly | Assumed |
| Public and media | Learn of it from the crowd's phones | Nothing | Assumed |

## Who decides, who acts, who is told

- Decides: the incident commander, full authority by scenario rule.
- Acts: operations director (gate policy, lights, PA), gate supervisor (each gate), police (streets), PA producer (the announcement), Sound Transit (trains).
- Told: stewards, fans, fire and rescue, public.

The gap the coach will probably find: the people who know which gates are open (gate supervisor) and the people who know which streets are safe (police) have no shared view, and the person with the clock (Titan Watch) has no stadium authority.
