# Interview: public-address and broadcast producer, Lumen Field

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Producer in the control room. Owns the bowl PA, the two video boards and the ribbon boards around the bowl. The only voice all 68,000 people hear at once. Reports to the operations director.

## Can see

The bowl from the control room window. The board feeds. A radio to the operations director. The broadcast feed, which is also what fans at home see.

## Cannot see

The concourses. The gates. Gorathos. Which gates the supervisor has held. The clock, until the director says a number.

## Questions and answers

**How fast can you tell 68,000 people one thing?**
Ten seconds to say it, ninety seconds before every section has heard it and looked up. The scripted emergency read is "proceed to the nearest exit". Tonight the nearest exit for half the building is the wrong one. Assume and label: ninety seconds to reach every section.

**Can you tell the west side something different from the east side?**
Not on the PA; it is one bowl. The ribbon boards are zoned by quadrant, so I can put "use east and south gates" on the west side and "walk, do not run" on the east. Nobody has ever written that message, and I would be writing it live. Assume and label: four zones, text only, sixty seconds to change.

**What happens when the director says kill the PA and the lights?**
The boards go dark too; they are the brightest thing in the building. Then the only instruction anyone has is the last one they heard, and 68,000 phones. I can leave the concourse lighting and the concourse PA, which is a separate circuit. I do not know whether that is quiet enough. Nobody knows.

**What have you been told so far?**
Stand by. That is all. The broadcast crew have the kaiju on their monitor from a camera on the waterfront. The fans have it on their phones. I am the only one in the building with nothing to say.

**What would help?**
The gate list, so I read what is true. If I say "east gates are open" and East 3 is held, I have just made a crush. And the message per zone written before tonight, not by me at the microphone.

## Coach observations

- The PA is one voice for one building, and the building has two safe sides and two unsafe. Per-zone instruction exists only on the ribbon boards, which nobody has scripted.
- The producer would announce from the gate list, which lives in the gate supervisor's head. An announcement from a stale list creates the crush the fire chief fears.
- Lights and PA are the attractor (A8) and the instrument. The producer has a partial answer, the concourse circuit, that nobody in the operations centre has asked about.
- "I read what is true" is the same ask as "one board, who said so and when", from the voice end.
