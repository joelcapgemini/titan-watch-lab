# Interview: stadium operations director, Lumen Field

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Director of event operations for the stadium operator. Owns the building on event night: lights, PA, every steward, every gate. Sits in the operations centre on the 200 level with the CCTV wall, the gate supervisor on channel 2 and the Titan Watch liaison at the next desk.

## Can see

The CCTV wall: bowl, concourses, gate lines. Gate counters, one number per gate, a minute behind. Radio to the gate supervisor, the PA producer and the section supervisors. The liaison's laptop, if the liaison turns it round.

## Cannot see

The street beyond the gate line. Gorathos, except through the liaison. Where the police have put their officers. Whether a train is at the platform.

## Questions and answers

**What happens in your room when the liaison says 11 minutes?**
I call the gate supervisor and ask which gates are open. I call the PA producer and tell them to stand by. I call the police lead and ask which streets I can send people into. That is three calls and it takes four minutes before anyone outside this room is doing anything different. Assume and label: four minutes.

**How long does a full clear take?**
Twenty-five minutes on a normal night with every gate open and people going the way they came in. Tonight half of them came in from the west and north, which is the water and downtown. If I close those gates I have half the exits and people walking the wrong way to find the other half. Call it forty minutes, which is not a number that matters with eleven. Assume and label: twenty-five with everything, forty with half.

**Have you ever held 68,000 people inside?**
For lightning, twice. "Stay in your seats, we will tell you when." It works because people trust the building against lightning. Nobody has drilled holding them against something that walks through buildings, and I would not know what to tell them to make them stay.

**What about the lights and the PA?**
The liaison says it goes toward noise and light. We are the loudest and brightest thing in the city. I can kill the floodlights and the bowl PA in thirty seconds. Then nobody can see a stair and nobody can hear an instruction. I do not know which of those kills more people.

**What would you want from a tool?**
One board. Every gate, open or held, which way it faces, who said so and when. The liaison's clock on the same board, with how old it is. Not the CCTV wall. I have a CCTV wall and it tells me nothing about which way is safe.

## Coach observations

- Four minutes of phone calls before action. The same lag as the Sea-Tac duty manager, in a building with a shorter clock.
- Twenty-five becomes forty becomes a number that does not matter. The hard choice is not empty or hold; it is which gates, and for whom, and Q4 stays open.
- Lights and PA are the stadium's own attractor and its only means of moving people. A8 is carried by this interview and checked by none.
- "One board, every gate, who said so and when" is a requirement if it survives the other interviews.
