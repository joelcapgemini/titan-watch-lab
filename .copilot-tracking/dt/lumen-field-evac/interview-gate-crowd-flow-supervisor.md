# Interview: gate and crowd-flow supervisor, Lumen Field

Label: role-play, not evidence. Pre-seeded by the facilitator. Method 1b.

## Who

Supervisor of the gate crews. Opens and closes every gate on event night by radio on channel 2. Walks the gate line; is rarely in the operations centre. Reports to the operations director.

## Can see

Whichever gate they are standing at. The gate crews' voices on channel 2. The queue on the concourse side of each gate. The counters, if someone reads them out.

## Cannot see

The street beyond any gate. Gorathos. The clock, except when someone says a number on the radio. What the PA is about to say.

## Questions and answers

**How do you hold or release a gate?**
I say it on channel 2. "West 2, hold." Whoever is on West 2 hears it and pulls the gate. Thirty seconds to hold, thirty to open, if the crew is there. There is no record. If somebody else says "West 2, open" a minute later, West 2 opens, and I may not hear it because forty people share that channel. Assume and label: thirty seconds, and I miss one call in five.

**Which gates face the kaiju?**
I have been told west and north open toward the water and downtown and that is where it is coming from. Nobody has shown me a map with it on. If it turns, I do not know which gates that changes. Assume and label: west and north face it, east and south do not.

**How many people can you put through east and south in nine minutes?**
Sixteen gates on those two sides. Say a thousand a minute a side when the queue is orderly, less when it is not. Eighteen thousand in nine minutes if everybody walks and nobody falls. Somebody always falls. Assume and label: fifteen thousand.

**What happens at a held gate when the crowd behind it knows what is outside?**
That is a crush. I have seen one at a held gate for ten minutes on a normal night with nothing behind it but rain. People push because the people behind them push. A held gate needs a reason on the board above it, and people need to see the next gate is open.

**What would help?**
A list of every gate with one word next to it, hold or open, that everybody at every gate can see, and that changes only when somebody with a name changes it. Right now the list is in my head and on a channel forty people are shouting on.

## Coach observations

- The hold-or-release decision exists, per gate, in the air on channel 2, with no record and no name. That is the audit gap on the scenario card seen from the gate line.
- Eighteen thousand becomes fifteen thousand becomes an unknown. Q4 stays open with a role-play range, and most of 68,000 cannot leave by any plan.
- The supervisor has never seen the track on a map. The gate facing question (A5) is carried by hearsay.
- "One word per gate, everybody sees it, changed only with a name" matches the operations director's board from the other end of the radio.
